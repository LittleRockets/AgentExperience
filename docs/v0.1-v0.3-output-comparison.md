# V0.1–V0.3 single-content output comparison

Date: 2026-08-24  
Model: `deepseek-v4-pro`  
Temperature: `0.0`  
Task: the same Chinese two-day New York itinerary request in all four arms

The Baseline arm used only the original system and user content and had no experience repository,
selection result or injected advice. V0.1 used direct legacy rule injection, V0.2 reproduced the
frozen ACTIVE-only retrieval plus rule-token-budget path, and V0.3 used the current Policy Object
hard filters, adaptive deterministic score and rule-token-budget path.

| Arm | Experience | Prompt tokens | Completion tokens | Total tokens | Latency (s) | Characters | Tables | Rubric |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Baseline | No | 84 | 1292 | 1376 | 27.210 | 2099 | 0 | 100 |
| V0.1 | Yes | 166 | 2465 | 2631 | 52.053 | 4002 | 2 | 100 |
| V0.2 | Yes | 174 | 1957 | 2131 | 42.420 | 3208 | 6 | 100 |
| V0.3 | Yes | 174 | 1355 | 1529 | 29.072 | 2332 | 2 | 100 |

Observed in this run:

- Baseline was already complete enough to hit the rubric ceiling, so this fixture cannot show a
  quality-score gain.
- V0.1 produced the longest and most expensive answer, adding 1173 completion tokens and 24.843
  seconds relative to Baseline.
- V0.2 produced the most tabular output and the most dynamic-information verification mentions.
- V0.3 was the most compact experienced arm: only 63 completion tokens and 1.862 seconds above
  Baseline in this single call.
- V0.3 selected one policy after all hard constraints passed. Its trace recorded selector version
  `0.3.0`, deterministic scorer, policy hash, component scores, rank and reason codes.

V0.2 and V0.3 delivered byte-for-byte equal effective prompts to the model in this fixture. Their
different generated text therefore cannot be attributed to the selection protocol: even with
temperature zero, the remote service does not promise identical generations. The protocol-level
difference is that V0.3 validates risk and other hard constraints and writes a decomposed,
replayable decision trace before returning the same bounded advice.

This is a single-content, single-call demonstration, not a statistically significant effectiveness
result. The rule rubric has a visible 100-point ceiling, and structural counts do not establish
travel-fact correctness. Reproduce with `examples/new_york_version_comparison.py`; raw outputs and
the complete JSON report are written under ignored `demo-output/`.
